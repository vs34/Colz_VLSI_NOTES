## Module map (high level)
- **Week 1**: Introduction, scaling (Moore's law, ICs), chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, lattice energy)
- **Weeks 2–3**: Quantum Mechanics foundations (Wave-particle duality, uncertainty principle, wavefunctions, postulates, Schrödinger's equation, particle in a 1D/2D box, quantum wells, tunneling)
- **Weeks 4–5**: Crystal physics and defects (Unit cells, Bravais lattices, Wigner-Seitz cells, reciprocal lattice, Brillouin zones, point/Schottky/Frenkel defects)
- **Weeks 6–7**: Electronic bands and density of states (Free electron theory, Kronig-Penney model, silicon/germanium band structures, direct/indirect semiconductors, effective mass, density of states (3D/2D/1D/0D), intrinsic and extrinsic carrier statistics, degenerate/non-degenerate)
- **Weeks 8–9**: Semiconductor contacts and heterojunctions (Homojunctions, heterojunctions, metal-semiconductor Schottky/Ohmic contacts, image-force lowering, Fermi-level pinning, tunnel diode, superlattices)
- **Weeks 10–11**: Carrier statistics and junction electrostatics (Intrinsic/extrinsic carrier concentration, charge neutrality, band bending, Poisson-Boltzmann equation, built-in potential, depletion approximation under reverse/forward bias)
- **Week 12**: Transport mechanisms (Drude model, drift, scattering, mobility, velocity saturation, velocity overshoot, diffusion, Einstein relation, PN junction biasing and IV characteristics)
- **Week 13**: MOS capacitor and MOSFET (Basic structures, band bending under bias, interface charge, threshold voltage, MOS capacitance, long-channel MOSFET current-voltage characteristics, transfer characteristics, leakage)
- **Reading**: Textbook chapter 5 (Chenming Hu) on MOS Capacitors
