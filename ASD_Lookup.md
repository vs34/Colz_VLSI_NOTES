# ASD Lecture Content Lookup

Course: Advanced Solid-State Devices (ASD) — Sneh Saurabh — Jan–April 2025.
Lookup of what each lecture PDF covers, derived from slide titles. Use to route questions to the correct file without re-scanning all PDFs.

## Module map (high level)
- **Weeks 1–2**: Basics of semiconductor physics (Energy bands, effective mass, E-k diagrams, density of states, Fermi-Dirac distribution, intrinsic and extrinsic carrier concentration, doping)
- **Weeks 3–4**: Carrier transport (Drift, mobility, scattering, conductivity, velocity saturation, diffusion, Einstein relation, excess carriers, recombination mechanisms, SRH theory, quasi-Fermi levels)
- **Weeks 4–6**: Governing equations and junction physics (Continuity/transport equations, Poisson's equation, ambipolar transport, PN junction electrostatics, ideal and non-ideal I-V characteristics, junction breakdown, metal-semiconductor junctions)
- **Weeks 7–9**: Field-Effect Transistors (MOSFET structure and gate/drain control, MOS capacitor electrostatics, flat-band voltage, parasitic charges, threshold voltage, C-V characteristics, body effect, small-signal models, frequency response)
- **Week 10**: Short-channel effects (Subthreshold current/swing, DIBL, hot electron effects) and BJT basics
- **Week 11**: BJT physics (BJT non-ideal effects, hybrid-pi model, device and circuit simulation)
- **Weeks 12–14**: Advanced devices and scaling (CMOS scaling limits, advanced architectures: FD-SOI, FinFET, GAA/Nanosheet, CFET, high-k dielectric, strain engineering, TFET, NCFET, 1D/2D channel materials)
